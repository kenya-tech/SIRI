THE SIRI INITIATIVE

Turning information into institutional intelligence.

SIRI is the digital research and intelligence platform of SOBO. It creates a structured environment for collecting student feedback, coordinating research, evaluating activities, monitoring concerns and preserving institutional knowledge.

One platform. Multiple perspectives.

Through integrated digital tools, SIRI helps transform scattered student experiences and research contributions into organised information that can inform representation, planning and decision-making.
